Who We Are

Rooted in Community, Growing with Purpose

From humble beginnings to certified excellence, we're committed to sustainable farming practices that nourish our community.

📖

Our Story

Founded on a passion for farming and community empowerment, Nkadimeng Agri began operations in 2021 and achieved official company registration in 2024. Rooted in Lynnville, Emalahleni, we're dedicated to producing premium organic poultry while creating sustainable opportunities for our community.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide the highest quality organic poultry products while promoting sustainable agriculture, supporting local economies, and maintaining the highest standards of animal welfare and environmental stewardship.

👁️

Our Vision

To become South Africa's leading provider of organic poultry, setting new standards for quality, sustainability, and community impact while inspiring a new generation of farmers.
