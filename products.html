FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between live and freshly slaughtered chickens?

Live chickens are sold as is for those who prefer to process their own poultry. Freshly slaughtered chickens are humanely processed, cleaned, and prepared for immediate cooking.

How do I store the chickens?

Freshly slaughtered chickens should be refrigerated and consumed within 2-3 days, or frozen for up to 6 months. Live chickens should be processed within 24 hours.

Do you offer delivery?

Yes, we offer delivery within the Emalahleni area for orders above certain quantities. Contact us for specific details.
